Kint\textbackslash{}\+Zval\textbackslash{}\+Representation\textbackslash{}\+Method\+Definition\+Representation\+::get\+Docstring\+Without\+Comments (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}{}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}

Returns the representation\textquotesingle{}s docstring without surrounding comments.

Note that this will not work flawlessly.

On comments with whitespace after the stars the lines will begin with whitespace, since we can\textquotesingle{}t accurately guess how much of an indentation is required.

And on lines without stars on the left this may eat bullet points.

Long story short\+: If you want the docstring read the contents. If you absolutely must have it without comments (ie render\+Value\+Short) this will probably do.
